Character and animation assets used in Burger Shift: Diner Edition

The human character is derived from the CC0 1.0 player_base.glb created by
project artist Atlas using MPFB2 / MakeHuman for UIC-campus-game:
https://github.com/sthak34-sudo/UIC-campus-game/blob/b93933d3ec1bc26332cab5bdb713830c6d6d8f41/client/public/models/characters/player_base.glb
SHA-256 of source: efc00efe4f8c1cb0f7fbe9cfbcead36ba10659fdd1e4772d74aac6e97c3c1d61

The idle, walk and run clips are derived from the CC0 1.0 Universal Animation
Library (Standard) by Quaternius, retargeted to this body by UIC-campus-game:
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
https://github.com/sthak34-sudo/UIC-campus-game/blob/b93933d3ec1bc26332cab5bdb713830c6d6d8f41/client/public/models/characters/player_anims_cc0.glb
SHA-256 of source: 737c79b1c776ca4d540d361bafd3a456004fa1c2c078d9eac7547e7247e0a59e

Source project attribution and CC0 declaration:
https://github.com/sthak34-sudo/UIC-campus-game/blob/b93933d3ec1bc26332cab5bdb713830c6d6d8f41/CREDITS.md

The meshes were reduced to two source LODs, the animation clips were selected
and combined, textures were decoded and resized, shirt lettering was removed,
and colour variations were made. The result was converted to Panda3D BAM.
CC0 1.0 legal text is in CC0-1.0.txt. No original Burger Shift: Team Rush
assets are used.
